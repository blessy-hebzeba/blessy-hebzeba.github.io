About Me

My Journey

I'm a Data Analyst based in the Netherlands with nearly a decade of experience across software QA, IT infrastructure, and data analytics — spanning India, the UAE, and now Europe. My path has never been linear, and that's exactly what makes it interesting.

My MSc thesis on predicting ejection fraction from echocardiograms using 3D CNNs and LSTM networks was my first deep dive into medical AI — and it lit a fire I haven't stopped feeding since. I'm now actively building toward a research career in AI-powered medical image analysis.
